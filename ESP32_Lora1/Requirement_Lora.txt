### 1. Power Management (B1 & B2)
- **B1 and B2:** Independent pumps. Managed by the system to ensure they do not run at the same time as other pumps/valves to prevent exceeding electric power limits.
- **Scheduling:** B1 and B2 each have their own independent Period 1 & Period 2 Start Time and Duration configured via Blynk.

### 2. Sequential & Pump Logic (B3-B7)
- **Group Scheduling:** B3-B7 share a combined Period 1 & Period 2 Start Time and Duration setting.
- **Board 3 (Pump):** Automatically activates when any worker relay on B4, B5, B6, or B7 is active.
- **Worker Group (B4-B7):** 
    - B4: 4 active relays
    - B5: 6 active relays
    - B6: 4 active relays
    - B7: 6 active relays
- **Sequence:** Only one relay from the entire group {B4, B5, B6, B7} is "nominally" active, but they utilize a 1-minute overlap.
- **Overlap:** 1-minute overlap between *every* relay in the sequence, whether on the same board or transitioning between boards (e.g., B4 Relay 4 overlaps with B5 Relay 1 for 1 minute).
- **Control:** Master calculates the full start/stop schedule for all boards (B1-B7) and broadcasts it. Slaves save this to NVM (Preferences) and execute independently using local RTC.

### 3. Manual Mode
- Triggered via Blynk (V107-V109).
- Target: Individual Relay ID (mapped to B4-B7).
- B3 (Pump) starts/stops automatically with the manual command.
- Manual commands are real-time and do not require NVM storage.

### 4. Safety & Persistence
- **Autonomous Operation:** If LoRa communication fails, all boards (B1-B7) continue their internal schedule from NVM memory using local DS3231 RTC.
- **Heartbeat:** Slaves report status to Master.
- **Emergency Stop:** Global broadcast.
- **OTA Support:** All boards support ArduinoOTA for future wireless updates.
- **Standby:** All relays OFF.
